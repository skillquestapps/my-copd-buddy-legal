Privacy Policy

My COPD Buddy · Last updated August 22, 2026

The short version. My COPD Buddy has no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours. What you enter stays on your iPhone. There is no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. The only information that ever leaves your phone is the ZIP code you type into the Air Quality screen, and only so we can look up the air quality for that area.

What the app keeps on your iPhone

Everything you enter is saved on the device itself:

  • Your medications and their schedules, including a photo of a medication if you choose to add one
  • Doses you record, and notes you add to them
  • Your care team contacts and your emergency contact
  • Questions you save for an appointment
  • Vaccination records and allergies
  • Oxygen readings you enter from your own pulse oximeter
  • Breathlessness grades you save
  • Flare-up records
  • Preferences: the name shown in your greeting, whether voice guidance is on, whether medication names are hidden on the lock screen, and your saved Air Quality ZIP codes

None of this is transmitted to us. We cannot see it, and there is nowhere for it to be uploaded to; the app has no backend service.

The one thing that leaves your phone

When you use the Air Quality screen, the ZIP code you type is sent to Apple's location lookup (to turn it into a location) and to the Open-Meteo air-quality service (to get the current reading for that location). Nothing else goes with it: not your name, not your health records, and no identifier for you or your device. Those services have their own privacy practices, and we don't control them.

What the app does not do

  • No account. You never create one, and there is nothing to sign in to.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking. The app contains no analytics tools, no advertising software, no crash reporting, and no third-party software of any kind.
  • No purchases. There is nothing to buy inside the app.
  • No measuring. The app does not read your oxygen level, your breathing, or any other vital sign. It records what you type in.
  • No health-app access. The app does not read from or write to Apple Health.

Reminders

Medication and Health Calendar reminders are scheduled and shown by your iPhone. They are never sent from a server, so no reminder, and nothing in it, travels over the internet. By default, Settings' "Hide details on the lock screen" switch keeps both kinds of reminder generic there — a medication name or whatever you typed for a calendar event won't be readable on a locked screen unless you turn that switch off.

Camera

The camera is used only if you choose to scan a prescription label, so you don't have to type the details. The text is read on your device, and nothing is saved until you confirm what it found. If you add a photo to a medication, that photo is stored with it on your iPhone.

Links to other websites

Some screens link out, to the American Lung Association, to airlines, and to medication manufacturers. Those pages open in a browser view inside the app, and once you're there you're on that organization's website under their privacy policy, not ours. My COPD Buddy is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any of them.

Sharing your own information

If you use a share option in the app, for example to send your medication list to a family member or your care team, you choose the recipient and the app you send it with. That is entirely under your control, and it happens only when you ask for it.

Children

My COPD Buddy is intended for adults managing their own COPD, or for someone helping them. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

Deleting your information

You can delete individual entries — medications, contacts, saved questions, vaccination records, oxygen readings, breathlessness grades, and flare-up records — at any time from the screen where they appear. Settings also has a "Delete All My Data" option that removes everything the app has saved in one step, after you confirm. Deleting the app from your iPhone removes everything it has saved, the same as this control does. Because none of it was ever sent to us, there is nothing for us to delete on our end and no request you need to make.

California privacy rights

SkillQuestApps is based in California, and this section describes your rights under California law (the CCPA/CPRA) whether or not you live there. We do not sell or share your personal information, and we never have — there's no data broker relationship or advertising network to sell it to. Because the app has no server and no account, we don't hold a profile of you to access, correct, or delete on your behalf; everything is already on your own device, under "Deleting your information" above. If you believe we're mistaken about any of this, contact us using the email below.

International users

If you're outside the United States, including in the EU, EEA, or UK, the same practices described on this page apply to you: nothing you enter is transmitted anywhere except the one ZIP-code lookup described above, and that only happens if you use the Air Quality screen. We don't operate servers outside your device, so there's no cross-border transfer of your information for us to describe beyond what Apple's own location lookup and Open-Meteo's service already involve, under their own privacy practices.

Changes to this policy

If the app ever starts doing something this page does not describe, adding an account, a sync service, analytics, or anything else that sends your information somewhere, this policy will be updated before that version ships, and the date at the top will change.
