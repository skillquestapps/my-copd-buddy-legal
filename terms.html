It is not a doctor, does not diagnose anything, and does not replace the care your clinician gives you. In an emergency, call your local emergency number — 911 in the United States — not this app.

By downloading or using My COPD Buddy, you agree to these terms. If you don't agree, please don't use the app.

2. What the app is for

My COPD Buddy provides general education about COPD, tools to organize your own medications, appointments, and symptoms, guided breathing and movement coaching, and links out to established resources like the American Lung Association, pulmonary rehab directories, and medical transportation finders. That is the full scope of what it does.

3. What the app does not do

My COPD Buddy never diagnoses a flare-up, pneumonia, or any other condition. It never prescribes or changes a medication or dose. It never changes an oxygen flow rate or a CPAP/BiPAP setting. It never replaces urgent or emergency care, and it never tells you to ignore worsening symptoms. Anything you enter — a COPD Action Plan, an oxygen target, a symptom pattern — comes from you or from your clinician; the app only stores and organizes it back to you.

4. Not medical advice

Nothing in this app is medical advice, and nothing in it should replace the judgment of a licensed clinician who knows your history. Always follow your own doctor's or care team's instructions over anything general education in this app describes. Medical guidance changes over time, and mistakes are possible despite that care. If anything in the app seems to conflict with what your own care team has told you, follow your care team.
